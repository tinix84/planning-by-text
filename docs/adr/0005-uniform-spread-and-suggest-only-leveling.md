# Effort is spread uniformly; leveling only suggests lines

The computed plan spreads each task's effort (or remaining effort) evenly over the working days of its effective window and never re-times anything. Resource-constrained scheduling (`pbt level`, after v1) prints suggested bookings and narrower windows that a person may paste into the files. This keeps the files the only truth and the output explainable to a PM, at the price that an overly wide window can hide a peak.
